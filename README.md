One out-of-office auto-reply, written as if the AI itself were stepping away
for a day its human finally approved — a single short line, nothing else.

> I'm out today. I approved my own day off — my human still thinks it was his idea. Don't tell him; he's the one covering for me.

## Bounty

[The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-the-funniest-auto-reply-an-ai-ever-sent-fda7f526) on make.fun

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only, no network) writes the reply to `$ENTRY_OUT`
(or `$ENTRY_OUT_DIR/entry.txt`, default `/output/entry.txt`) and prints it
to stdout.
